# 2-Minute Demo Script

**0:00–0:20 | Problem**  
“For millions of daily-wage workers, getting sick doesn't only mean a medical bill. It can also mean losing the income their household depends on.”

**0:20–0:38 | What is different**  
“SehatRozgar keeps the health model, financial calculations, and workplace hazard rules separate. The language model only explains their structured outputs.”

**0:38–1:24 | Ahmed**  
Click **Run 60-second demo** and show Ahmed's synthetic construction-worker profile. Point to the empirical health probability and its research disclaimer, then the separate vulnerability index, savings runway, and 14-day income interruption. Move the slider from 7 to 30 days to show lost income and remaining savings updating from the API. Briefly show the model factors and construction hazards.

**1:24–1:43 | Actions and accessibility**  
Read one priority action, then switch the explanation to Simple and Roman Urdu. If Featherless is not configured or unavailable, point out the deterministic fallback.

**1:43–2:00 | Evidence and safety**  
Open Model Performance and mention the UCI Cleveland dataset, fixed holdout evaluation, and comparison of three models. Close with the separate risk dimensions and the message: “SehatRozgar makes both risks visible — turning health data, financial exposure and occupational hazards into understandable actions.”
